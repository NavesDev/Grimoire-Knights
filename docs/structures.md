# Structures

`.nbt` templates generated by script; the script is the source, as with the [models](models.md).

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `art/structures/run.py` | Source: each template block by block |
| `art/structures/nbt.py` | NBT reading and writing (gzip) |
| `art/structures/source/` | Downloaded ready-made builds, the base of some structures ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-community-builds.md)); don't edit |
| `data/grimoireknights/structure/` | Generated; used by the game |

Generate (from the repo root): `python3 -I art/structures/run.py`. The output is deterministic.

## Templates
| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| `tower/grimoire_tower` | Grimoire Tower: the Observation Tower (25×87×25 base) with its stone shaft (15×15, 44 high) turned into a library: bookshelves from the floor up to block 40 with wood and glowstone bands every 8, open windows, grimoires (`floating_grimoire`) circling the central column (no collision or selection), Grimoire Altar in the middle, priest beside it, door to the south. The observation room at the top is closed off, decoration only. Outside the shaft is `structure_void` (keeps the terrain). `/gk tower place` |
| `starter_village/center` | Start piece of the starter village: 39x39 plaza with the tower in the middle (7 blocks of clearance around the top of the tower). Jigsaws: vanilla plains streets to the west, north and east (`minecraft:street`); the church to the south, facing the tower door |
| `starter_village/church` | Church (12×22, 32 high) with a priest; the pews are beds (optional respawn). Jigsaw `grimoireknights:church` past the door steps, which face the tower |
| `empty` | 5x5 floor for GameTests |

## Starter village
- Structure `grimoireknights:starter_village` (jigsaw, `size` 6): starts at the tower plaza; streets and houses come from the vanilla plains pools.
- `structure_set` in concentric rings with `distance` 0 and `count` 1: a single one, at chunk 0,0 (or the nearest village biome, up to 112 blocks away). `/locate structure grimoireknights:starter_village` finds it.
- On world creation, `world` puts the spawn on the edge of the plaza, near the tower, and sets `spawnRadius` to 4: players spread over a 9x9 square of the plaza without reaching the tower (the tower's top room, wider than the shaft, starts 6 blocks from spawn). Obstructed spawn: vanilla uses the highest free block. Existing worlds don't change; `/gamerule spawnRadius` restores vanilla.
- Safe zone: hostile mobs don't spawn inside the village pieces (natural spawns, patrols and phantoms) and raids don't start there: the Bad Omen of whoever walks in is spent without becoming a raid.
