## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- **Stage 1: ceremony, fire grimoire and mana bar** (#2).
  - Pure rules for the lottery, calling, mana, casting, burn and quests.
  - Core: registries, commands root, configs, networking and test helpers; creative tab with the altar and the priest spawn egg.
  - Datapack quest engine, with progress, events and commands.
  - Grimoire: fire element, calling, priest ceremony at the altar and floating grimoire; free element choice screen with one card per element; advancement with the grimoire's picture on receiving it; grimoire on the HUD.
  - Ceremony: the grimoire grows at the bookshelves, flies slower to the player and vanishes on arrival.
  - The floating grimoire grows and shrinks with a sparkle and is hidden in first person.
  - Abilities: mana, casting on the Z key and the fireball with burn stacks; mana HUD left of the hotbar with the Z slot cooldown; the fireball explodes on hit without breaking blocks and fades out at the end of its range.
  - VFX: own runtime for Blockbench particles; fireball explosion and grimoire sparkle.
  - World: Grimoire Tower, starter village at spawn and safe zone (no patrols, phantoms or raids); every player spawns on the village plaza, with spawn radius 4; tower and church rebuilt from community builds; tower grimoires shuffled, circling the middle and untouchable, without the five-leaf one; the calling trail stays until the player reaches the tower.
  - Priest with its own model (#3), staying at its post.
- **GeckoLib 3D models** generated by script in Blockbench (#3, PR #1): grimoire, fireball, mages, swords, broom, griffin, altar, buildings, mantle, banner, demon form and player poses.
- `client2` Gradle run to test LAN multiplayer with a second player (`Dev2`).

### Fixed
- Casting denied to spectators and dead players, failed casts reported and burn ticks fixed.
- The calling counts only health taken and stays private; the no-magic ceremony is safe.
- Starter village guaranteed, without patrols and phantoms, keeping the bonus chest.
- Grimoire icon turned so its clover cover faces front.
- The calling trail points at the tower instead of the structure start.

### Documentation
- ADRs for the stage 1 architecture, Blockbench particles played by an own runtime (0004) and structures based on community builds (0005).
- Mana HUD, GUI icons, element choice screen and attachment sync in the spell flow.
